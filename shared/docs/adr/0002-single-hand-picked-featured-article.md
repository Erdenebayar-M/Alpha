# One hand-picked Featured article, not a ranked queue

At most one Article is Featured, and staff choose it explicitly; featuring an Article un-features the previous one, and unpublishing it leaves no Featured article, with the site falling back to a display rule instead of the backend promoting a replacement. We rejected a ranked queue where #2 auto-promotes when #1 leaves: choosing "the best article" is an editorial decision made at a point in time, and a queue would make that choice weeks early and put an unreviewed Article in the most prominent slot without anyone noticing.

## Consequences

- A future popularity ranking is a separate concept (computed, not chosen) and should get its own name rather than reusing "featured". It could supply the site's fallback when no Article is Featured.
